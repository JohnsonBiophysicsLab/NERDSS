    Every angle on this page is computed by the same arithmetic as the simulator: transform() rotates the axis onto +z, both operands are projected onto the xy-plane, Vec3D::angle_between() returns the unsigned angle, and the sign is flipped when the projected cross product has a positive z component. θ comes straight from acos and is always in [0, π]; φ and ω are dihedrals in (−π, +π] — the endpoint is reported as +π, never −π.
