Interactive tools

The five association angles — θ1, θ2, φ1, φ2 and ω — are the fiddliest part of writing a parameter file. Both tools are built from the simulator's own measurement code, so they agree with what NERDSS does, including where an angle is undefined and must be written as nan, and where ω is read from the normal vectors instead of the centre-of-mass arms. They run in the browser; nothing is installed or uploaded.

Angles → geometry

Angle explorer

Set the five angles and watch the bound geometry they produce on a 3D model, with each sign convention and singularity exposed as you move them. Start here to understand what an assocAngles line means.

Open the angle explorer

Geometry → angles

Angle solver

Work backwards: give the bound-state coordinates and the two normal vectors, and read off the five angles together with the assocAngles line to paste into your parameter file.

Open the angle solver
