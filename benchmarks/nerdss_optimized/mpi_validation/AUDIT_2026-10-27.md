The second pass over `nerdss_mpi` on the 1.4 branch, after PR #80 and #87
were merged (see `README.md` for the first pass and its tooling).  Branch
`claude/mpi-audit-1-4`, cut from `4017ece8`.  Everything below was measured
on one shared 10-core Mac; the correctness results do not depend on the
machine's load, the timings were taken alone.

### Restarts at np>1 (new)

`nerdss_mpi -np N -r <file>` with N > 1 was refused.  067ed391 lets each
rank cut its own share out of the file it read, through `prepare_rank_data()`,
the splitter rank 0 runs for a new run, which had to learn what a new run
never needs (nothing is bound at step 0):

* a complex travels whole: members outside the rank's visible columns are
  added, outside every cell, as a received member whose column lies beyond
  the rank is held, and bound partners and the reweighting history are
  mapped to the rank's numbering;
* ownership of a straddling complex is settled by a rule every rank
  evaluates alike (the rank whose owned columns hold the lowest-id member;
  the position rule gave it an owner on each side), and `isGhosted` follows
  the stamp;
* the implicit lipid's free sites per state are split with the telescoping
  rounding; the tallies the checkpoint writer sums over the ranks stay on
  rank 0 and start from zero elsewhere; ranks above 0 seed their stream with
  seed + rank when no saved state is found;
* every molecule in the right edge columns is marked not yet received back
  together with its whole complex, as the send does.  Without that, a dimer
  across the boundary that the neighbour dissociates at the top of the first
  step came back as its edge member alone and the other member's copy kept
  the bond (hetTrimer np=2: STRUCTURE VIOLATION at the first exchange; found
  with the id tracer of bb8ab467).

`restart_np_check.sh`: hetTrimer at np=2/3/4 and implicit_lipid at np=2/3
restart from the row the np=2 run wrote at the checkpoint (the split is
lossless), run to the end under both checkers with no report, keep every
molecule in the final histogram block, and hetTrimer's continuations agree
with the serial build's continuation of the same checkpoints over 8 seeds
(bound pairs at the last row: np=2 60.0 vs serial 59.9, np=3 57.0 vs 59.9,
z = +0.03 and -0.96).  The one-rank path is untouched (`checkpoint_check.sh`:
exact continuation).

Limits: a complex wider than two columns (more than 2 rMaxLimit) can have a
member two ranks away from its owner, which the neighbour-only exchange does
not reach, as for a new run once such complexes form.  A saved RNG state is
rank 0's only.

## Not a defect: unimol_state_change at np=2

The tip audit flagged `B(thr~P)` at np=2 (5 runs, |t| 5.8), and every
re-measurement on the fixed builds kept it: 21 runs |t| 3.0, 16 runs |t| 3.3
(147.6 `B(thr~U)` against 150.8 serial), with np=1 and np=3 fine and `A`,
which carries the same reaction, fine.  It was chased to the end because a
bias in a first-order draw would be wrong physics everywhere:

* checkpoints every 2500 steps (16 runs) showed the merged counters equal
  to the molecule states in every one of 64 snapshots, so no state change
  was lost or double-counted in an exchange;
* the excess was spread over the whole box, not at the rank boundary;
* a tally build (draws and fired draws per state, not committed) put the
  realised rates at 1026 / 2899 s^-1 against 1000 / 3000 for B at np=2 (A's
  3000 s^-1 reaction: 2999), i.e. the draws themselves came out 6% off in
  ratio, which no bookkeeping can do;
* and then with fresh seeds (101-140 at np=2, 101-124 serial): 49.6 +- 0.6
  against 50.5 +- 0.8, z = -1.0.  Every earlier ensemble had used seeds 1-16
  or 1-21, and `nerdss_mpi` is reproducible per seed, so the re-measurements
  were the same trajectories again, not new evidence.  One cell in 3.3 at
  p = 0.002 among the sixty or so case/np cells of an audit is what chance
  delivers.

The lesson for the harness is in `README.md`: a re-test of a flagged cell
must take seeds the audit did not use.

## Still open

* **Restarts at np>1**: complexes wider than two columns; the RNG state is
  rank 0's (ranks above 0 reseed with seed + rank).
* **Performance**: the exchange (above).  `find_molecule()` /
  `find_complex()` are linear scans per received molecule and per partner;
  the receives wait on the neighbour's whole half-step.  Nothing on this
  branch changes that.
* **Refused at start-up, by design**: the column rule (every rank between
  two others needs 3 columns of width rMaxLimit: `trimer` and
  `michaelis_menten` above np=2, `unimol_state_change` above np=3), and
  compartments (no transmission reactions under MPI).
* **Output merging** still covers copy numbers, the complex histogram and
  PDB/CIF frames only; the other outputs stay per rank.
* **Known leftovers from earlier sessions** (two owners after a cross-rank
  association, double binding of a stripe molecule, `ownerRank` on recycled
  slots): none produced a report in these suites (ownership checked every
  step at np=2/3/4 over 3 seeds, plus ASan), and nothing was changed for
  them; they stay as described in `README.md`.
* **The deferral's cost** (a complex the neighbour bound into takes no
  first-order draw that step) is unchanged and unmeasured beyond the
  copy-number comparison.
* **Standalone branch**: wrong at np=4 and at np=2 for two models,
  slower than serial everywhere; not a basis for anything.
