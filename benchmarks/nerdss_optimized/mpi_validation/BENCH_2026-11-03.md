the wide-box scaling table (`SCALING_2026-10-30.md`) on the 1.4 tip after

## What is left at np=1

`sample` (1 ms, 8 s) of rev_3D at np=1 on the tip, 56 x 21 x 21 sub-cells
for 1000 molecules, main thread 6015 samples, self time:

| function | mpi14 np1 | serial14 |
|---|---|---|
| `SimulVolume::update_memberMolLists` (MPI overload) + `add_member` | 21.1% + 6.8% | 4.6% + 1.8% |
| `remove_empty_slots` (every MPI step) | 11.4% | 0 |
| `measure_separations_to_identify_possible_reactions` | 11.5% | 14.3% |
| `Complex::propagate` + `update_properties` | 8.5% | 19.8% |
| `gsl_ran_gaussian_ziggurat` | 4.4% | 12.3% |

Per step: 0.707 ms at np=1 against 0.303 ms serial.  Half of the gap is
the cell-list rebuild: the MPI overload of `update_memberMolLists()`
(class_SimulVolume.cpp, the one taking `MpiContext&`) clears
`memberMolList` and `typeMask` of **every** sub-cell with
`for (auto& subBox : subCellList)` on each of its three calls per step,
while the serial overload calls `clear_member_lists()`, which walks the
occupancy mask and touches only the cells that hold a molecule.  On this
grid that is 3 x 24696 cell clears per step for 1000 molecules.  The fix
is to call `clear_member_lists()` in the MPI overload too (exact: the
result is the same empty grid) and to look at whether the second and third
rebuilds per step can be incremental.  `remove_empty_slots()` is the other
tenth: it compacts the molecule and complex vectors every step; running it
only when a slot was actually emptied (deletions are counted) is also
exact.  These two account for the rev_3D, hexamer and clathrin np=1
ratios that did not move with the pair walk; they are proposal item 1's
"remaining 2-4%", which on sparse boxes is 30-40%.

## Code: the standalone branch against 1.4

* `src/mpi/` entered this line with the "NERDSS 1.2.0" import
  (c1da91b4, 2024-11-26) from the standalone branch's state of that day,
  already edited (10 shared files, +1376/-774 lines against the standalone
  at that commit).  Since then 1.4 has 92 MPI commits; the shared files
  have grown from 3509 to 5603 lines, and 1.4 adds
  `region_split.cpp`, `defer_edge_reactions.cpp`, the two checkers,
  `mpi_context.hpp`, `io_mpi/merge_outputs.cpp`, `rank_tallies.cpp` and
  `write_gathered_restart.cpp` (2341 lines with no counterpart).
* The standalone branch has four commits after the import: 684e4d69
  (set `isRightGhost` on arrival), 72162338 (update member lists and region
  flags before the species file at start-up), 73d3fe5f (update them again
  after the receive from the right; stop copying the four
  `isLeft/RightEdge/Ghost` flags from the old slot on deserialize), and
  f1dd0590 (a `debug_dir` of binaries and inputs).  All three code changes
  patch the edge/ghost flag bookkeeping that 1.4 removed: ownership
  (`ownerRank`) travels with the complex and `derive_ghost_from_ownership()`
  recomputes the flags after every exchange.  Nothing on the standalone
  branch is left to port.
* Against its own upstream, the standalone branch forked from
  `nerdss_development` master in July 2021 (merge base 7cc0fc31) and never
  merged it back: 335 commits of its own, 318 master commits it lacks.

## Status

* `nerdss_mpi` (1.4 tip): correct against the serial ensemble at every
  rank count the column rule admits, faster than the audit at every np>1,
  and faster than the serial build from np=2 on a 42-column box
  (hetTrimer_wide 0.79x, 0.50x, 0.41x at np=2/4/8).  On the 8-column

  fixable exactly.  Communication per rank is a constant that caps the
